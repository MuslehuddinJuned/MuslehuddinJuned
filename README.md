I'm an **Industrial & Production Engineer turned ERP developer and solution architect**, with **13+ years of professional experience** across manufacturing, retail, packaging, textiles, furniture, and education.

My approach to ERP is **domain-first**: understand how the business actually operates, then design the technology around it.

- 🚀 **Implementation ownership** — development → deployment → training → support
